About Mackenray

Who We Are

We're a Bahrain-based AI automation and web development studio. We work exclusively with service businesses — clinics, contractors, and local teams — who need their digital presence to actually generate revenue, not just look good.

We work remotely with service businesses across the Gulf region and beyond.

Every build is handled personally. No outsourcing, no templates, no fluff.
